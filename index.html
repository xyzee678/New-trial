🎯 What is Lecture Planner Pro?

A smart, mobile-friendly study management app that automatically organizes your course lectures into daily schedules based on your study speed. It helps you track what you should be doing (allocated) versus what you actually did (done), so you always know your exact status.

💡 Core Purpose: "Stop wondering what to study today — let the app tell you, track your progress, and show you where you're falling behind or ahead."

✅ Why Is This Useful?

📌 For Students
  • Know exactly what to study each day
  • See your completion trend across days
  • Track when you actually completed vs when planned
  • Identify weak spots and improve
📌 For Professionals
  • Organize training modules or courses
  • Track learning progress over time
  • Share progress reports with managers
  • Maintain daily learning habits
🎯 The Big Picture: Instead of guessing what to study, the app calculates your daily schedule. Instead of wondering if you're on track, the battery gauges show your exact progress. Instead of forgetting what you moved around, the app tracks copies and shows you the real picture of allocated vs done.

📖 How to Use — Step by Step

🚀 Getting Started (First Time)

  1. 1 Upload Your Course: Go to Upload tab → enter course name → paste lectures in format 1 Lecture_Name 45 → click Upload
  2. 2 Or Test with Dummy: Click 🎲 Dummy Data to add 2 sample courses instantly
  3. 3 Set Your Study Speed: Go to Edit tab → select your course → set Speed (e.g., 120 mins/day) and Start Date
  4. 4 Your schedule is now ready! Go to Timetable to see your daily plan

📅 Daily Workflow (Every Day)

  1. 1 Check Dashboard: See your 5-battery trend → shows completion for 2 previous days, today, and 2 next days
  2. 2 View Today's Schedule: Go to Timetable → click today's date → see what lectures are assigned
  3. 3 Study & Mark Complete: Check the box for each lecture you complete → row turns green ✅
  4. 4 Use Batch Actions: Click "✅ Tick All" to mark all lectures on a date complete, or "⭕ Untick All" to reset
  5. 5 Set Done Date for All: Click "📅 Set Done Date" to set the same done date for all completed lectures on that date
  6. 6 Track Progress: Dashboard updates in real-time — battery fills up as you complete more

🔄 When Things Change (Copying, Rescheduling, Resetting)

  1. 1 Need to Copy a Lecture to Another Day? Click "← Copy" or "Copy →" on any lecture → creates a copy on the target date (original remains)
  2. 2 Want to Remove Copy Tracking? Click 🧹 (Clear Copy Stamp) → removes all "Copied from/to" tags
  3. 3 Need to Change Speed for One Day? Click any date in Timetable → enter custom speed → schedule recalculates instantly
  4. 4 Want to Start Fresh? Go to Edit → click "🔄 Reset Course" → clears all progress (ticks, copies, done dates) — course data remains
  5. 5 Need to Rename a Course? Go to Upload → click ✏️ next to course name → enter new name → updates everywhere

📊 Analyzing Your Progress

  1. 1 Dashboard: See 5-day battery trend at a glance
  2. 2 History Tab: See allocated dates (when you should have done it) vs done dates (when you actually did it)
    Rows color-code: 🟢 Green = Done early, 🔴 Red = Done late, 🔵 Blue = On time
  3. 3 Expected vs Actual Hours: History shows total expected hours vs actual hours completed → see your efficiency
  4. 4 Battery Grid per Date: History shows mini batteries for each date in your selected range

✅ The Focus Formula:

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use the Focus Course: Select your main course once — it auto-appears in all dropdowns (⭐ indicator). No repetitive selections!
  • Batch Update Done Dates: If you studied an entire day's lectures on a different date, use "Set Done Date for All" to update everything at once.
  • Copy for Extra Practice: Use "Copy →" to duplicate a lecture to the next day for additional review. The original remains.
  • Clear Copy Stamps: When you no longer need to track where a lecture came from, click 🧹 to clean up the tags.
  • Sunday = Pink: Sundays are highlighted in pink — plan your weekends differently.
  • Custom Speeds: Set 180 mins on Sundays, 60 mins on busy days — the schedule adjusts automatically.
  • Reset When Needed: If you want to start fresh without re-uploading, use "Reset Course" — keeps your lectures, clears progress.
  • Export Often: Auto-export runs at 12 PM daily, but you can also manual export anytime with Ctrl+S.
  • History = Your Reality Check: Allocated vs done dates show the truth — use it to adjust your planning.
